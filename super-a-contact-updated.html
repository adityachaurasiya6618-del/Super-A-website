Aditya's World

Aditya is a 14-year-old student from Azamgarh. He enjoys learning, technology and exploring unusual things. He is intelligent and curious, but at the beginning he has no idea that his life is about to change.

The Mystery

Something happened. Nothing stayed normal.

A mysterious event gives Aditya extraordinary abilities. At first, he does not understand them. The early story focuses on discovery: what changed, why it happened, and whether he can control it.


The identity of the hero develops gradually. The name, costume and full purpose come later — as part of Aditya's journey rather than being revealed all at once.

About the Project

Super A is being developed as a modern Indian superhero universe. The website is designed to grow with the comic: new pages, characters, villains, locations and story arcs can be added without changing the core design.

  • Original Indian superhero setting
  • Character-first storytelling
  • Mystery and gradual power discovery
  • Expandable comic archive
  • Responsive design for phones, tablets and computers
